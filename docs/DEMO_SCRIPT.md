# CampaignLab 90-second demo

**0:00–0:12 — The problem**  
“Marketing teams rarely lack dashboards. They lack a reliable way to turn messy evidence into the next decision. I built CampaignLab to calculate first, explain second and show where the evidence stops.”

**0:12–0:30 — Strategy Lab**  
Enter one concrete decision. Show the structured brief: the move, why it wins, assumptions, risks, counterargument and the evidence that would change it.

**0:30–0:55 — Evidence Lab**  
Load the campaign experiment. Show recognition before method selection. Run one eligible method, then show the blue evidence verdict, one chart and optional statistical detail.

**0:55–1:18 — MMM Beta**  
Load the known-answer MMM demo. Move from readiness and validation to contribution, response curves, the budget scenario and green allocation verdict. State that observational attribution is not automatically causal.

**1:18–1:30 — The differentiator**  
“Python owns the numbers. AI challenges and explains them. The decision record preserves assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty and what happened next.”
