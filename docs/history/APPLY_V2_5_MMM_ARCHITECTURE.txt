CampaignLab V2.5 — Decision Architecture + MMM Lab Special

BASE: CampaignLab V2.4 NAV/FORM build.

WHAT THIS RELEASE DOES
- Rebuilds Analytics Directory around business decision families instead of mixed statistical categories.
- Moves z-tests, Welch t-tests, Fisher, ANOVA, confidence intervals, etc. underneath the workflows that actually use them.
- Adds shared Data Gate readiness contracts for prediction and forecasting, and uses the same readiness philosophy in MMM.
- Adds a dedicated Lab Special route/workspace for Marketing Mix & Budget Optimizer (Beta).
- Adds deterministic MMM readiness, adstock, saturation, constrained model fitting, chronological holdout validation, contribution reporting and budget-scenario optimization.
- Adds a reproducible 156-week MMM demo dataset.
- Adds 3 MMM regression tests; total test suite is now 37 tests.

IMPORTANT MMM GUARDRAIL
The MMM Lab Special is Beta. Its output is observational/model-based contribution, not proof of causality. High-stakes allocation should be calibrated with experiments or credible quasi-experimental evidence when possible.
